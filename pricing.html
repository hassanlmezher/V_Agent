FAQ

Questions, Answered

How does the monthly plan work?add

You subscribe to a plan, add requests to your board, and we work through them in order. Each plan sets how many requests we work on at the same time.

Can I pause or cancel?add

Yes. You can pause or cancel at any time. Unused days in a paused billing cycle carry over to when you resume.

What counts as a "request"?add

A request is a single, clearly scoped task — a new landing page, an agent workflow, a video edit. Larger projects are broken into several requests.

Do you take one-off projects?add

Yes. If a monthly plan isn't the right fit, tell us about your project and we'll send a fixed quote.

Who owns the work?add

You do. Everything we create for you — code, designs, prompts and footage — belongs to you once it's delivered.
